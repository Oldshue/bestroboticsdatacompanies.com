About Best Robotics Data Companies

Best Robotics Data Companies is an independent research site. It answers one question: which companies are the best sources of robotics and physical-AI training data right now?

Who runs it

The site belongs to a small network of independent guides, each focused on one topic in AI and data. We don't publish author bios because the work is desk research, not expert testimony. We don't claim credentials, industry awards or special access.

How we work

  • We research from public sources, mainly each company's official website, and link to those sites.
  • We do not test products, and we never claim hands-on experience.
  • We do not publish user reviews or testimonials of our own.
  • Our method is set out on the methodology page.

Independence

Companies cannot pay to be listed or to rank higher. The links to vendor sites are plain links, not affiliate links.
